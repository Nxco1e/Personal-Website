Outside the Classroom

Life beyond academics

Travel

I love traveling because it gives me perspective, curiosity, and a deeper appreciation for different cultures and ways of living. I have been to more than 10 countries, and each trip has taught me something new about resilience, independence, and seeing the world in a broader way. One of my most meaningful experiences was traveling solo to Iceland, where I had the chance to explore new places on my own and build confidence.

Fitness

I value health and fitness because it directly impacts my mindset, energy, and productivity. I believe taking care of your body is not just about appearance, but about discipline, confidence, and being able to show up at your best in every area of life. I try to go to the gym 4–5 times a week because it helps me manage stress, improve my focus, and build habits that support long-term growth.

Concerts

Music has always been a big part of my life, and concerts are one of my favorite ways to experience energy, emotion, and community. Some of the shows that stood out to me include Alex Warren, Bruno Mars, and DJ Fisher.

Skydiving

I have gone skydiving before, and it was one of the most thrilling and confidence-building experiences I have ever had. It reminded me that growth often comes from stepping outside of your comfort zone and choosing to experience life with courage.

Big Snoopy Fan

I’m a big Snoopy fan. I love the comfort, nostalgia, and personality that comes with it. Snoopy is one of those things that feels instantly recognizable, cheerful, and a little bit nostalgic — and that’s exactly why it means so much to me.

I am a Twin

I am a twin, and that has shaped so much of my life. Being a twin means growing up with built-in companionship, shared memories, and a constant reminder that life can be even more fun when you have someone who truly understands you.

Big Sweet Tooth

Now I have a big sweet tooth. I love desserts, nostalgic treats, and anything that feels fun, comforting, and worth enjoying. There is something about a sweet treat that makes life feel a little brighter.
